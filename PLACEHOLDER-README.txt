This folder is where your real files go:

- profile.jpg  → your profile photo (square, 500x500px or larger recommended)
- resume.pdf   → your resume, exported as a PDF

Until you add them, the site shows a placeholder image for your photo, and the
resume download buttons will point to a file that doesn't exist yet — add
resume.pdf here and they'll work automatically.

You can delete this text file once you've added your own assets.
